Content marketing plan

Helpful guides that lead to confident purchases

NovaTech sells a small catalog, so our content has to do the explaining a salesperson would do in a store: what to look for, what to avoid, and which product fits.

01

Primary audience

Canadian college and university students, aged 18–24, who rely on a phone and laptop all day and want dependable accessories on a student budget.

02

Secondary audience

Young professionals and hybrid workers, aged 22–35, building a portable home-and-office setup who value compatibility and fast charging.

03

Brand message

“Smart gear. Better everyday.” Practical tech accessories, explained clearly and sold honestly, with no hype and no clutter.

04

Voice & tone

Friendly, knowledgeable, and plain-spoken. We explain specs in everyday terms, give honest trade-offs, and never overstate what a product does.

Publishing rhythm

One guide every two weeks

Each post is 150–200 words, ends with a link to a relevant product, and is shared on social media the day it goes live. Upcoming topics: earbud battery care, choosing a smartwatch for fitness, and protecting your phone on a budget.

How we measure it

Success metrics

Organic search visits, blog-to-product click-through rate, add-to-cart rate from blog visitors, and social shares per post. Once the store is live, these would be tracked through Google Search Console and consent-based analytics.

Social media promotion strategy

Short, useful, and consistent

We go where our audience already spends time and turn each blog guide into short-form posts. Every NovaTech page includes share links for X, Facebook, Reddit, and WhatsApp, and every blog post has its own share button.

IGInstagram
3 posts / week + Stories. Clean product photos, carousel tips, and Reels showing accessories in a student’s day.

2 videos / week. 20–40 second “how to choose” and “did you know” clips based on each guide.
XX (Twitter)
4–5 posts / week. Quick tips, new-guide announcements, and replies to tech questions.

2 posts / week. Longer guide summaries and back-to-school offers aimed at parents and older shoppers.

Search engine optimization

On-page SEO checklist

Every page was reviewed against the checklist below. Primary keyword focus: tech accessories Canada, supported by product keywords such as wireless earbuds, power bank, smartwatch, and USB-C hub.

CheckStatusHow it is implemented
Unique meta title on every pageDoneKeyword first, brand last, about 60 characters or fewer (for example “Shop Tech Accessories: Earbuds, Power Banks & Hubs | NovaTech”).
Unique meta descriptionDoneAbout 130–150 characters, including the page keyword and a reason to click.
Keyword usageDoneTarget keywords appear in the H1, the opening copy, product names and descriptions, and blog headings without keyword stuffing.
Descriptive image alt textDoneAll six product images have alt text naming the product and its key feature; decorative copies use empty alt text.
One H1 and logical headingsDoneEach page has a single H1 followed by H2 and H3 sections.
Canonical URLsDonerel="canonical" on every indexable page prevents duplicate-URL issues.
Social sharing metadataDoneOpen Graph and Twitter Card tags with a 1200 × 630 share image.
Structured dataDoneJSON-LD OnlineStore on the home page and a Product list with CAD prices on the products page.
Sitemap and robots.txtDonesitemap.xml lists the indexable pages and robots.txt points to it. Cart and checkout pages are marked noindex.
Internal linkingDoneEach blog guide links to the related product; navigation and footer link every key page.
Mobile-friendly and fastDoneResponsive layout, a viewport tag, lightweight SVG images with set dimensions, and no third-party scripts outside checkout.
